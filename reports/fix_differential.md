| analyzer-pairs | 1 | 0 | 1 | 6 |
| monoliths/expression-calculator | 14 | 11 | 3 | 10 |
| monoliths/graph-paths | 15 | 12 | 3 | 10 |

| monoliths/library | 16 | 14 | 2 | 9 |
| monoliths/text-analyzer | 11 | 8 | 3 | 9 |
